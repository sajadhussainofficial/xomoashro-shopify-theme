![Anaemia](https://xomoashro.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Anaemia.jpg)

Anemia, a condition where your body lacks enough healthy red blood cells to carry sufficient oxygen to its tissues, is a prevalent health concern. For those grappling with anemia, the telltale sign is low hemoglobin levels, leaving them feeling consistently tired and weak. Women, in particular, face a higher risk of anemia due to monthly blood loss during menstruation. Notably, iron deficiency anemia stands out as a significant contributor to maternal morbidity and mortality. In the pursuit of combating anemia, enter Shilajit – a potential game-changer. This natural substance emerges as a beneficial ally in the fight against anemia by serving as an excellent iron supplement.
