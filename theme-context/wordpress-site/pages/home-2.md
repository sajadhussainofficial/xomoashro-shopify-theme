[What is Shilajit?]()

Shilajit is a naturally occurring resin found in the high mountain ranges of Central Asia, particularly in the Himalayas. It is a complex mixture of organic and inorganic compounds, including fulvic acid, humic acid, minerals, and trace elements. Shilajit has been used in traditional medicine for centuries for its purported health benefits. {hidden}

[How do I take Shilajit?]()

Shilajit is available in various forms, including powder, capsule, and liquid extract. The recommended dosage of Shilajit varies depending on the form and individual needs. It is generally recommended to start with a low dose and gradually increase it as tolerated. {hidden}

[Are there any side effects of Shilajit?]()

Shilajit is generally considered safe for most people when taken in recommended doses. However, some people may experience mild side effects, such as stomach upset, diarrhea, and skin rash. It is important to talk to your doctor before taking Shilajit if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have any underlying health conditions. {hidden}

Yes, Shilajit is renowned for its energy-boosting properties. Regular use may help combat fatigue, increase energy levels, and promote overall vitality. {hidden}
