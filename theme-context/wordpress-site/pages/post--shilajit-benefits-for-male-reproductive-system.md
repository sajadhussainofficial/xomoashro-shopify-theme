## What Are the Side Effects of Shilajit?

- Iron Levels and Hemochromatosis: Animal studies suggest that shilajit may elevate iron levels, posing a potential risk for individuals with hemochromatosis—a condition characterized by excessive iron in the blood. Those with this condition should exercise caution and refrain from shilajit supplementation until more human studies are conducted.

- Hormonal Impact: Shilajit has been associated with alterations in hormone levels, particularly a significant increase in total testosterone. Individuals with pre-existing hormonal imbalances or those undergoing hormone-related treatments should consult healthcare professionals before considering shilajit supplementation to ensure it aligns with their health needs.

- Contamination Concerns: Raw or unprocessed shilajit may carry health risks, as it could be contaminated with heavy metals or fungi. To mitigate these concerns, it is advisable to choose shilajit supplements from reputable sources that undergo thorough processing to ensure quality and safety.

- Research and Informed Choices: Due to the limited research on the safety of shilajit, individuals contemplating its use should stay informed about the latest findings. Consulting healthcare providers before incorporating shilajit into a wellness routine is crucial to making informed choices and prioritizing overall health and well-being.
