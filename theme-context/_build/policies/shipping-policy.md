## Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses across Pakistan. We do not ship outside Pakistan at the moment.

## Payment

You pay in cash when your order arrives (cash on delivery). Please keep the amount shown at checkout ready for the courier.

## Order confirmation

After you place your order you will receive a confirmation by email. We may also call or send you a WhatsApp message to confirm your address before we send the parcel. Please keep your phone switched on so the courier can reach you.

## Delivery time

Most orders are delivered within 5 working days. Deliveries to remote areas, and orders placed during public holidays or bad weather, can take longer. We will let you know if there is a delay.

## Delivery fee

The delivery fee, if any, is shown at checkout before you place your order. You will never be asked to pay more than the total shown at checkout.

## Tracking your order

You can follow your order from the Track your order page or by messaging us on WhatsApp with your order number.

## If we cannot deliver

If the courier cannot reach you or the address is incomplete, they will try to contact you again. If the parcel cannot be delivered, it comes back to us and we will get in touch to arrange another delivery.

## Damaged or wrong parcels

Please check your parcel when it arrives. If it is damaged, or something is missing or wrong, send us a photo on WhatsApp or by email within 48 hours of delivery and we will put it right. See our Refund policy for details.
