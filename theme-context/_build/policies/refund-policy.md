## Our 7-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with your order, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will refund the price of the product. This applies to opened and unopened jars, once per customer.

## Damaged, leaking or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, leaking, incomplete or wrong, send us a photo on WhatsApp or by email within 48 hours of delivery. We will replace the item or refund it in full, including any delivery fee, and you will not need to pay to send anything back.

## How to ask for a refund

1. Message us on WhatsApp or email us with your order number and the reason.
2. We will reply with the return address and arrange the return with you.
3. Send the jar back with its lid closed, in its packaging if you still have it.

## Return postage

For refunds under the money-back guarantee, the cost of sending the jar back is paid by you, unless the item was damaged or wrong.

## How you are paid

Because orders are paid in cash on delivery, refunds are paid by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. We send the refund within 7 working days of receiving the returned jar, or of confirming a damaged or wrong item. The delivery fee is not refunded, except for damaged or wrong items.

## Exchanges

We do not exchange items for a different product. If you want a different size, return the jar for a refund and place a new order.
