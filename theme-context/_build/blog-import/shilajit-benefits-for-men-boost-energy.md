<p>Men take shilajit mainly for energy, strength and vitality. Of all the claims, these have the most research behind them, because most human studies on shilajit were done in men. Even so, there are only a handful of trials, and they are small. The results are encouraging, not conclusive.</p>
<p>Here is what the studies found, what they did not show, and how to take shilajit sensibly. For the basics, see <a href="/blogs/guides/what-is-shilajit">What is shilajit?</a></p>
<h2>Energy and tiredness</h2>
<p>Shilajit has a long reputation in Ayurveda as a <em>rasayana</em>, taken to restore strength. A review in <em>Phytotherapy Research</em> reported animal and human data supporting its use as a "revitaliser" that may reduce fatigue, and linked this to its main active compounds, fulvic acid and dibenzo-α-pyrones. The same review stressed that more well-controlled human studies are needed.</p>
<p>In practice, many men take it each morning as part of their routine. If you feel tired most of the time, though, see a doctor first: tiredness can be a sign of something that needs treatment.</p>
<h2>Strength and recovery</h2>
<figure><img src="{{file:xomoashro-journal-shilajit-benefits-for-men-muscle-recovery.webp}}" alt="A smiling athletic man holding a jar of shilajit" width="1024" height="1024"></figure>
<p>The best study on exercise was a placebo-controlled trial in 63 active young men. They took 250 mg or 500 mg a day of a purified shilajit extract, or a placebo, for eight weeks. Among the stronger half of the group, those on 500 mg lost less of their strength during a tiring leg exercise test (about 9% compared with about 16% on placebo). The lower dose made no difference.</p>
<p>That is a real but narrow finding: one study, one dose, one kind of exercise. Shilajit is not a substitute for training, food and rest, but it may be a reasonable extra for active men.</p>
<h2>Testosterone</h2>
<p>A randomised, placebo-controlled trial gave men aged 45 to 55 a purified shilajit extract, 250 mg twice a day, for 90 days. Their total and free testosterone rose significantly compared with placebo. A 2024 systematic review of "testosterone boosters" found that most products do not work, but rated purified shilajit as "possibly effective" for older men with low testosterone.</p>
<p>The evidence is in older men, with a purified extract, from a small number of studies. It does not show that shilajit raises testosterone in young, healthy men. We cover this in detail in <a href="/blogs/guides/exploring-shilajits-impact-on-testosterone">Does shilajit raise testosterone?</a></p>
<h2>Fertility</h2>
<p>A small study gave processed shilajit to 35 men with low sperm counts for 90 days. The 28 who finished had higher sperm counts and better motility at the end. The study had no comparison group, so we cannot tell how much of the change was due to shilajit. Read more in <a href="/blogs/guides/shilajit-benefits-for-male-reproductive-system">Shilajit and male fertility</a>.</p>
<h2>Heart, immunity and other claims</h2>
<p>You will see claims that shilajit protects the heart, lowers blood pressure, boosts immunity or treats anaemia. These come from laboratory and animal studies, not from good human trials. Shilajit should not be used to treat any of these conditions. If you have a heart condition or high blood pressure, speak to your doctor before taking it.</p>
<h2>What helps more than any supplement</h2>
<ul>
<li>Regular exercise, including strength training</li>
<li>Seven to nine hours of sleep</li>
<li>A healthy weight and a balanced diet</li>
<li>Managing stress</li>
<li>Regular check-ups, especially after 40</li>
</ul>
<p>Shilajit may add a little on top of these. It cannot replace them.</p>
<h2>Who should be careful</h2>
<p>Speak to your doctor before taking shilajit if you take regular medication, have heart, kidney or liver disease, have a condition that causes too much iron in the body (such as haemochromatosis), or have a hormone-related condition or are on hormone treatment. Stop and get advice if you feel unwell.</p>
<h2>How to take it</h2>
<p>Take a pea-sized amount of resin, dissolved in warm water, milk or tea, once a day. Start small and keep to the amount on the label. See <a href="/blogs/guides/how-to-take-shilajit-dosage-tips">How to take shilajit</a> for details.</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>What are the main benefits of shilajit for men?</h3>
<p>The best evidence, from small trials, is for reduced loss of strength during exercise and higher testosterone in older men. Energy is the most common reason men take it.</p>
<h3>How long does shilajit take to work?</h3>
<p>The studies ran for eight to twelve weeks. Give it a few weeks before you judge.</p>
<h3>Does shilajit increase stamina?</h3>
<p>One trial found that men on 500 mg a day kept more of their strength during a tiring test. That is one study, so treat it as promising rather than proven.</p>
<h3>Can I take shilajit with protein or creatine?</h3>
<p>There is no known problem, but check with your doctor or pharmacist if you take other supplements or medicines.</p>
