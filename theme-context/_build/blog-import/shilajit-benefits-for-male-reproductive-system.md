<p>Shilajit has been used for male vitality in Ayurveda for centuries, and two small human studies suggest it may help sperm count and testosterone. But the studies are small, one had no comparison group, and none looked at erectile problems. Shilajit is not a treatment for infertility or sexual problems.</p>
<p>This guide sets out what the research found and when to see a doctor instead. For the basics, see <a href="/blogs/guides/what-is-shilajit">What is shilajit?</a></p>
<h2>Sperm count and quality</h2>
<p>In a study published in 2010, 35 men with low sperm counts took 100 mg of processed shilajit twice a day for 90 days. The 28 men who completed the study had, on average, higher total sperm counts (up 61%), better sperm movement and more normally shaped sperm at the end. Their testosterone also rose.</p>
<p>These results are interesting, but the study had no placebo group. Sperm counts naturally vary from one test to the next, and men in a study often change their habits too, so we cannot say how much of the improvement came from shilajit. Larger, controlled trials are needed.</p>
<h2>Testosterone</h2>
<figure><img src="{{file:xomoashro-journal-male-reproductive-system-body.webp}}" alt="Illustration of a glowing human figure with a jar of shilajit" width="668" height="536"></figure>
<p>The stronger evidence on hormones comes from a randomised, placebo-controlled trial in men aged 45 to 55. After 90 days of purified shilajit extract, 250 mg twice a day, total and free testosterone were significantly higher than with placebo, while the hormones that control testosterone production stayed normal. See <a href="/blogs/guides/exploring-shilajits-impact-on-testosterone">Does shilajit raise testosterone?</a> for the details and limits.</p>
<h2>Erectile problems</h2>
<p>You will see shilajit sold as a natural remedy for erectile dysfunction. There are no good human studies showing that it works for this.</p>
<p>More importantly, erection problems can be an early sign of heart disease, diabetes or high blood pressure. If you have them, please see a doctor. It is a common problem, doctors deal with it every day, and the right treatment depends on the cause.</p>
<h2>Does shilajit increase size?</h2>
<p>No. There is no evidence that shilajit, or any supplement, increases penis size. Products that promise this are not telling the truth.</p>
<h2>Energy, stress and libido</h2>
<p>Many men say they feel more energetic on shilajit, and a review of the research describes it as a traditional "revitaliser" with some animal and human data on reduced fatigue. Feeling less tired can help libido, but there are no good trials of shilajit on libido itself. Stress, poor sleep and alcohol all lower sex drive, and tackling them helps more than any supplement.</p>
<h2>When to see a doctor</h2>
<ul>
<li>You and your partner have been trying for a baby for a year without success (or six months if your partner is over 35).</li>
<li>You have erection problems that keep coming back.</li>
<li>You have pain, swelling or a lump in the testicles.</li>
<li>You have signs of low testosterone, such as low sex drive, tiredness and loss of muscle.</li>
</ul>
<p>A doctor can arrange a semen test or blood tests and find the cause. Shilajit can be discussed alongside that, not instead of it.</p>
<h2>Possible side effects and cautions</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hormones:</strong> shilajit can raise testosterone. If you have a hormone-related condition or are on hormone treatment, speak to your doctor first.</li>
<li><strong>Iron:</strong> animal studies suggest it may raise iron levels. Avoid it if you have a condition such as haemochromatosis.</li>
<li><strong>Contamination:</strong> raw or poorly made shilajit can contain heavy metals or fungi. Only take purified shilajit from a seller who can show a lab report.</li>
<li><strong>Medication:</strong> check with your doctor or pharmacist if you take any regular medicine.</li>
</ul>
<h2>How to take it</h2>
<p>Take a pea-sized amount of resin, dissolved in warm water, milk or tea, once a day, and keep to the amount on the label. The studies above ran for 90 days. See <a href="/blogs/guides/how-to-take-shilajit-dosage-tips">How to take shilajit</a>.</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Does shilajit improve sperm count?</h3>
<p>One small study without a comparison group found higher sperm counts after 90 days. It is a promising sign, not proof.</p>
<h3>Can shilajit treat erectile dysfunction?</h3>
<p>There is no good evidence that it does. See a doctor, because erection problems can be a sign of other health conditions.</p>
<h3>How long should I take shilajit for fertility?</h3>
<p>The study ran for 90 days, which is about how long it takes the body to make new sperm. If you are trying for a baby, speak to a doctor as well.</p>
<h3>Does shilajit increase size?</h3>
<p>No. No supplement does.</p>
