<p>Possibly, in some men. The best evidence is one randomised, placebo-controlled trial in which men aged 45 to 55 took a purified shilajit extract for 90 days and their testosterone rose compared with placebo. A 2024 review of testosterone supplements rated purified shilajit as "possibly effective" for older men with low testosterone. There is no good evidence that it raises testosterone in young, healthy men.</p>
<p>Here is what the studies found, why the results need care, and what else affects testosterone. For the basics on shilajit, see <a href="/blogs/guides/what-is-shilajit">What is shilajit?</a></p>
<h2>The main study</h2>
<p>Published in <em>Andrologia</em> in 2016, the trial gave healthy men aged 45 to 55 either purified shilajit, 250 mg twice a day, or a placebo for 90 consecutive days. Neither the men nor the researchers knew who was taking which.</p>
<p>At the end, the shilajit group had significantly higher total testosterone, free testosterone and DHEAS (another hormone made by the adrenal glands) than the placebo group. The hormones that signal the body to make testosterone, LH and FSH, stayed within normal levels.</p>
<h2>What the review says</h2>
<p>A systematic review in 2024 looked at 52 studies of products marketed as "testosterone boosters". Its main finding was that most of them do not raise testosterone. A few ingredients were rated "possibly effective" for particular groups, and purified shilajit extract was one of them, for men with late-onset hypogonadism (low testosterone in older age).</p>
<p>That is a cautious but meaningful result. Shilajit is one of the few "booster" ingredients with any controlled evidence at all.</p>
<h2>What the evidence does not show</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Young men:</strong> the trial was in men aged 45 to 55. We do not know whether younger men would see the same change.</li>
<li><strong>Raw resin:</strong> the trial used a standardised, purified extract in measured doses. Results may differ with other products.</li>
<li><strong>How much it matters:</strong> a higher number on a blood test does not automatically mean you will feel different, build more muscle or have a higher sex drive.</li>
<li><strong>Long-term use:</strong> the trial lasted 90 days.</li>
</ul>
<p>A smaller study in men with low sperm counts also found testosterone rose after 90 days of processed shilajit, but it had no placebo group, so it adds little certainty. We cover it in <a href="/blogs/guides/shilajit-benefits-for-male-reproductive-system">Shilajit and male fertility</a>.</p>
<h2>Do you actually have low testosterone?</h2>
<p>Low testosterone can cause low sex drive, tiredness, low mood, and loss of muscle and strength. So can many other things, including poor sleep, stress, depression and thyroid problems. The only way to know is a blood test, usually taken in the morning. If you have symptoms, see a doctor rather than guessing with supplements.</p>
<h2>What else raises testosterone naturally</h2>
<p>Whatever you decide about shilajit, these habits have the strongest effect on your hormones:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>A healthy weight.</strong> Excess body fat lowers testosterone, and losing weight often raises it.</li>
<li><strong>Strength training.</strong> Regular resistance exercise supports muscle, bone and hormone health.</li>
<li><strong>Enough sleep.</strong> Short sleep lowers testosterone. Aim for seven to nine hours.</li>
<li><strong>Less stress.</strong> Long-term stress raises cortisol, which works against testosterone.</li>
<li><strong>A balanced diet</strong> with enough protein, vegetables and whole grains, and less alcohol.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Who should not take shilajit for testosterone</h2>
<p>Speak to your doctor first if you are on testosterone or other hormone treatment, have or have had prostate problems, are trying for a baby, take regular medication, or have a condition that causes too much iron in the body. Shilajit is not suitable for teenagers.</p>
<h2>How to take it</h2>
<p>Take a pea-sized amount of resin, dissolved in warm water, milk or tea, once a day, and keep to the amount on the label. The trial ran for 90 days. See <a href="/blogs/guides/how-to-take-shilajit-dosage-tips">How to take shilajit</a>.</p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Does shilajit increase testosterone?</h3>
<p>One placebo-controlled trial found it did in men aged 45 to 55. The evidence in younger men is lacking.</p>
<h3>How long does shilajit take to raise testosterone?</h3>
<p>The trial measured the effect after 90 days of daily use.</p>
<h3>Is shilajit a steroid?</h3>
<p>No. It is a natural mineral resin, not a hormone or steroid.</p>
<h3>Can I take shilajit with testosterone treatment?</h3>
<p>Only if your doctor agrees. Do not combine them without medical advice.</p>
